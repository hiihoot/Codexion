##  ./codexion 5 3000 200 200 200 10 400 fifo
                      Cooldown check: after a coder releases a dongle, that dongle must not be
                      taken again until 400 ms have elapsed. By reading the timestamps, verify
                      that no "has taken a dongle" line for a given dongle occurs within 400 ms
                      of its release. time_to_burnout (3000) is generous, so no coder should burn
                      out and the run should complete.

./codexion 5 3000 200 200 200 10 800 fifo   then   ... edf
                      Run the exact same parameters once with fifo and once with edf. The large
                      cooldown (800) forces coders to queue for the dongles. Compare the order in
                      which the waiting coders are granted the dongles: under edf, a coder closer
                      to its burnout deadline may be served ahead of one that requested earlier,
                      so the grant order can differ from the strict arrival order seen with fifo.
